About Lumos

Lumos is a lightweight per-application brightness controller for Windows.

What it does:
- Runs silently from the system tray.
- Detects the app you are actively using.
- Learns brightness changes you make manually.
- Restores the saved brightness when you return to that app.
- Keeps profiles and settings stored locally on this PC.

After installation:
- Lumos starts in the tray when launched.
- Open the tray menu to view settings, profiles, pause automation, or exit.
- Windows startup can be enabled from Lumos settings.

Privacy:
- Lumos does not require an account.
- Lumos does not upload your profiles or settings.
- Brightness profiles are saved locally.
